#intro
Principal software engineer based in Irvine, California

I have spent 12+ years shipping production systems for Amazon Prime Video, Bethesda, Lionsgate, CarParts.com and Renaissance Learning, where the platform I worked on served 44 million students and teachers daily. Today I am a Senior AI Software Engineer at Cisco and I consult independently through RADFAB.

My work sits at the point where an AI prototype has to become a product: multi-agent architecture, least-privilege tool access, evaluation pipelines, and fine-tuning when a local model beats an API call. I care most about the unglamorous half: whether you can prove the system got better, and whether anyone can audit what it did.

I speak regularly at the OC AI Tinkerers Guild at Chapman University, mentor engineers 1:1, and I am working through a Bachelor of Liberal Arts in Computer Science at Harvard Extension School. Outside of that: competitive baking, rock climbing, and a snowboard season pass I take seriously.

I am open to the right full-time role, but most of my work comes through consulting engagements.
